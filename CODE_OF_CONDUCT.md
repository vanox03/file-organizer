# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community.

Be considerate, give constructive feedback, respect differing viewpoints, and
focus discussions on improving the project. Harassment, personal attacks,
discrimination, and publishing another person's private information are not
acceptable.

Project maintainers may remove comments, commits, issues, or other contributions
that violate these standards. Reports can be sent privately to the repository
owner through GitHub. Maintainers will review reports fairly and protect
reporters' privacy where possible.

This policy applies in project spaces and when representing the project publicly.
